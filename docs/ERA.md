# Era rules

NFL REWIND begins in 1966, the Super Bowl I season. Seasons use 14 games through 1977, 16 from 1978 through 2020, and 17 from 2021 onward. The 1982 and 1987 strikes are reserved for the simulator's schedule exceptions.

Before 1970 the leagues display as AFL and NFL. Each league sends its champion to the Super Bowl, with no wild card. 1970–1977 uses divisional and conference playoffs; 1978–1989 uses a 10-team field; 1990–2019 uses 12; 2020 onward uses 14. 2002 introduces eight divisions of four and Seattle moves to the NFC West.

The simulator intentionally keeps passing volume, efficiency, sacks, interceptions, rushing touchdowns, and tight end receptions era-aware rather than using modern stat distributions.
