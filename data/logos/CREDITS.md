# Club marks

The badges in `public/logos` are original designs in each club's period colors, generated by
`scripts/build-logos.mjs`. They are not the NFL's official logos.

- Mascot silhouettes (`glyphs.json`): [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net) by Lorc,
  Delapouite and contributors, licensed [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/),
  via the `@iconify-json/game-icons` package.
- Letter marks: Big Shoulders, Erica One, Young Serif and Lora, all under the SIL Open Font
  License (license files in `fonts/`), converted to outlines with opentype.js.

Rebuild: `node scripts/build-logos.mjs`
